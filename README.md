# MOSAICS Studio: all-atom natural-move Monte Carlo on modern additive force fields in the browser

Folorunsho Bright Omage and Peter Minary
Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford

This repository holds the complete sources of the preprint, the scripts that
generate its tables and figures, and the evidence trees behind every number in
it. The compiled manuscript is `main.pdf`; the Supporting Information is
`si.pdf`.

MOSAICS Studio itself runs in the browser at
<https://users.ox.ac.uk/~coml1155/mosaics-studio/> (a temporary address; the
page moves to the MOSAICS project site, <https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/mosaics/>, on
release). Its source is at <https://github.com/omagebright/mosaics-studio>.

## What the paper reports

* MOSAICS, the natural-move Monte Carlo engine, evaluates modern additive
  force fields at all-atom resolution: AMBER ff14SB and ff19SB (with its CMAP
  correction maps) for protein; parmbsc0, parmbsc1, OL15/OL3, OL21/OL3 and
  OL24/OL3 for nucleic acids; and CHARMM36 for nucleic acids.
* Single-point energies were compared with sander 24.0, OpenMM 8.5.2 and
  GROMACS 2025.4 on eleven systems of 56 to 6,139 atoms, in 26
  system-and-force-field combinations. Nineteen of the 26 agree with every
  available reference to within 0.001 kcal/mol; the median largest gap is
  0.00037 kcal/mol.
* Analytic forces were checked against central differences in 61 sweeps, and
  the cost of one Monte Carlo step was measured.
* MOSAICS Studio runs the engine, compiled to WebAssembly, and its own
  preparation and analysis pipeline inside the visitor's browser tab. A
  documented minimisation reproduces between the native and WebAssembly
  engines.

## Layout

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `main.tex`, `sections/` | the manuscript |
| `si.tex` | the Supporting Information |
| `tables/` | generated by `make_tables.py` from `data/` |
| `figures/` | figure PDFs and PNGs, the scripts that draw them, and the PyMOL renders |
| `data/` | `MANIFEST.json` (the eleven systems) and `MATRIX.json` (the 26-cell energy matrix) |
| `evidence/` | first-generation evidence: 1efs and the capped peptide, term by term, with every engine's output |
| `evidence-v2/` | second-generation evidence: one directory per system, with topologies, inputs and outputs for every engine |

## Building

```sh
sh build.sh
```

`build.sh` regenerates the tables and compiles both documents with
[tectonic](https://tectonic-typesetting.github.io/). Any XeLaTeX
installation with `newtx` and TeX Gyre Heros also works; pdfLaTeX falls back
to Helvetica.
