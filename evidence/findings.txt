Findings
========

Every entry has an identifier that does not change, what was wrong, how it was
found, and whether it is closed. Fixed entries stay here so the next reader
knows the ground has been checked.

F-01 to F-14 are energy findings. F-15 onwards cover forces, the converter,
and the structures the checks are run on. Nothing is removed when it is fixed:
a closed entry is how the next reader knows the ground has been checked.

An identifier is never reused. If an entry turns out to be two findings, the
second one takes the next free number rather than a letter on the first.

F-01  fixed   The engines use different electrostatic constants
--------------------------------------------------------------
sander was 0.064 kcal/mol away from OpenMM on 1efs while reading exactly the
same file. It is not a disagreement about the force field. sander uses an
older value of the constant that turns charge into energy; OpenMM and GROMACS
use the current one and agree with each other to ten digits. The constants
were measured rather than assumed, by putting two charges a known distance
apart. All electrostatic numbers are now corrected to one constant before
comparison. See engines.txt.

F-02  fixed   The two sides were reading different coordinates
-------------------------------------------------------------
MOSAICS read the structure file, the reference engines read the topology file,
and the two store positions to different numbers of decimals. The largest
difference was 0.0005 Angstrom. On the peptide that moved the short-range
energy by 0.573 kcal/mol, because that term rises as the twelfth power of
distance. It looked exactly like a broken force field. Both builders now write
the reference positions from the structure MOSAICS reads, and refuse to
continue if the two disagree.

F-03  fixed   DNA parameters were being applied to RNA
----------------------------------------------------
On 1efs, MOSAICS was 0.47 kcal/mol high on the torsion energy for both
parmbsc1 and parmbsc0. parmbsc1 is a DNA force field. Its files are stacked on
top of an older general set, and where both define the same four atom types
the later one wins, which is correct for DNA. 1efs also contains RNA, and four
of those entries need different values for RNA. The file had nowhere to keep
both, so the RNA strand was given the DNA values. Every force field that
passed carried a per-molecule label on each entry; neither failing one carried
any. The two files now carry the labels, and the error falls to 1.0e-04 and
3.6e-05.

F-04  fixed   Three files rounded a constant that did not need rounding
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The factor that reduces the electrostatic energy between atoms three bonds
apart is exactly 5/6. Three files stored it to seven or ten digits instead.
That shifted the 1efs energy by 1.0e-04 kcal/mol and made the two families of
files disagree on a quantity that does not depend on which of them is loaded.
Predicted and measured agree to seven digits. All files now carry the full
value and all four now return the same number.

F-05  fixed   An RNA atom was labelled with a DNA-only type
---------------------------------------------------------
Both files gave the RNA 5' carbon a type that parmbsc1 defines for DNA only.
The type is identical to the ordinary one in every respect except torsions, so
no energy changed when it was corrected, but the label was wrong and it is the
last piece of an over-application that was already half undone.

F-06  closed  The ParmEd conversion route collapses ff19SB's correction maps
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
GROMACS first appeared to disagree with the other three programs on this term
by 0.30 kcal/mol. It is not a disagreement about physics and not a fault in
the conversion tool.

ff19SB gives every kind of residue its own correction map. On this peptide the
four residues carry four different maps, differing by as much as 4.8 kcal/mol.
The GROMACS topology used here is produced from the AMBER topology by the
ParmEd command in engines.txt, and that route writes [ cmaptypes ] keyed on the
five backbone atom types spanning phi and psi. All four residues share those
five types, so the four per-residue maps collapse onto one: the conversion
keeps the first and applies alanine's map to the other three.

This is a property of the conversion route as it was used, not a statement
about what the GROMACS file format can hold. Published GROMACS ports of
amino-acid-specific CMAP force fields introduce residue-specific backbone atom
types so that each map gets its own type quintuple. That workaround was not
attempted here, so nothing in this work establishes that GROMACS cannot hold
these maps; what is recorded is what this route did.

F-09 is the related limit in the current MOSAICS deck format, and it is a
different claim: there the map depends on the residue that FOLLOWS the one
being corrected, which the deck format has no way to express at all.

The cell is left blank in results.txt rather than filled with a number. The
ff19SB result is measured against sander and OpenMM, which agree with MOSAICS
to 5.7e-08, and every other GROMACS value on that structure agrees with the
rest to about 1e-06.

F-07  open    OL24/OL3 cannot be built here
------------------------------------------
Three parameter files are needed and are not redistributable.

F-08  open    CHARMM36 for DNA and RNA has nothing to be checked against
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The terms convert. The converter emits the Lennard-Jones from the element
that holds it, 807 Urey-Bradley entries into the bend record's own bond slot,
and 188 harmonic impropers as \pot_type{harm}, none of which needs an engine
change. Counts are pinned in tests/test_openmm_xml_to_mosaics.py.

A reference energy now exists. charmm36.xml itself still cannot produce one --
its five nucleic templates each declare one external bond, on O3' and not on
P, and none of its 107 patches is a 5-prime patch, so it describes
mononucleotides and cannot build a strand. charmm36_2024.xml can, and does:
3011.9072066874 kcal/mol on the committed RNA control, term by term, in
charmm36.txt C-08.

The reference is built on a 5-prime-OH terminus, because that is the only kind
this file can express. Every patch that ends a chain removes the phosphate and
every patch that keeps the phosphate leaves an external bond unsatisfied.

What is still missing is the deck. charmm36_2024.xml declares no impropers in
the file -- they are generated when it is loaded -- so a deck for this release
has to be read out of the built System rather than out of the XML.

F-09  open    CHARMM36 for protein cannot be expressed
-----------------------------------------------------
Its correction maps depend partly on the residue that follows the one being
corrected. The file format selects a map by the residue itself. Supporting
this changes the format and the engine, so it is a decision to be taken, not
a conversion to be run.

F-10  open    Two structures have no AMBER cross-engine energy comparison
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
dna_acgt and rna_acgu carry no cross-engine energy comparison under any of the
six AMBER combinations. They are not outside the comparison altogether: both
are checked at the energy level against OpenMM under CHARMM36 nucleic
(charmm36_dna_acgt.tsv, charmm36_rna_acgu.tsv) and both are swept at the force
level under OL21/OL3 (forces/). What is missing is the AMBER energy row.

F-11  open    One term is untested
--------------------------------
The peptide is built in an idealised shape that sits exactly at the minimum of
its out-of-plane terms, so both GROMACS and MOSAICS report zero for them. A
deliberately distorted copy is needed.

F-12  note    The reference topologies carry a small known bias
------------------------------------------------------------
Every equilibrium angle written by the AMBER topology builder is the published
value multiplied by a seven-digit approximation of pi/180. MOSAICS reads the
exact value, so here the references carry the error, not MOSAICS. It is worth
1.8e-04 kcal/mol on 1efs and is the largest remaining difference on every row
that passes. It is reported, not corrected: rewriting a file that AMBER
produced would make the comparison less faithful to AMBER.

F-13  note    MOSAICS rounds three physical constants
----------------------------------------------------
Three constants compiled into the engine are rounded versions of the standard
values. This is the whole of MOSAICS's remaining electrostatic difference from
OpenMM, 1.5e-03 kcal/mol on 1efs. Reported, not changed.


F-14  fixed   The reference had been adjusted toward the deck it tests
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A reference adjusted toward the deck it is meant to test cannot measure that
deck.

The topologies committed under examples/1efs_heteroduplex/topology/ were built
by tleap and then edited: RNA C5' renamed CT -> CI on both parmbsc1 and
parmbsc0, and RNA C3' CT -> CE on parmbsc1, so that the reference would agree
with the MOSAICS RTF.

Every 1efs comparison made against those files was therefore partly circular.

The bsc1 and bs0 presets in tools/build_1efs_amber_reference.py build stock and
rename nothing. Where the resulting atom types differ from the RTF, that
difference is the measurement. F-05 is what that change exposed.

The edited topologies are still in the repository. They are inputs to the
example, not references, and must not be used as references.

F-15  fixed   The CHARMM36 deck had no van der Waals at all
--------------------------------------------------------------
A CHARMM XML puts the charge in NonbondedForce and the Lennard-Jones in a
LennardJonesForce of its own. The converter read both from NonbondedForce,
where charmm36.xml writes epsilon="0.0" sigma="1.0" for all 412 types. Every
one of the 85,078 ~inter_parm lines it produced said \eps{0}\sig{10}.

Not CHARMM-specific: it would have broken any XML with that layout. No AMBER
output changes, checked by regenerating ff14SB, OL15/OL3 and OL21/OL3 with both
converters and diffing all fifteen files.

F-16  open    Four Urey-Bradley constants are negative and cannot be carried
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
charmm36.xml gives P-ON2-P and its P3 variants a negative Urey-Bradley force
constant. CHARMM allows that. database.cpp:706 rejects fk_bond < 0 and stops
the whole deck, not one angle.

They are pyrophosphate linkages, not phosphodiesters, so they are off the
nucleic path. The converter drops them and counts them into the manifest as
urey_bradley_dropped_negative rather than losing them silently.

Whether MOSAICS should accept a negative 1-3 constant is a question about the
engine, not about the conversion.

F-17  open    A committed control has two hydrogens 0.060 Angstrom apart
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
examples/pure_terminal_controls/dna_acgt.pdb, residue DG 3. H5' and H5'' are
each 1.089 and 1.090 Angstrom from C5', which is correct, and 0.060 Angstrom
from each other, which makes the H-C-H angle 3.15 degrees instead of 109.5.

Invisible to every energy check: the pair is 1-3, which the non-bonded sum
excludes, so only the bend term sees it and the bend term had no independent
reference. The symptom is visible once measured -- this 126-atom 4-mer has a
bend energy of 508.62 kcal/mol and the 818-atom 1EFS heteroduplex has 220.56.

rna_acgu.pdb and 1efs.pdb are clean; checked, not assumed.

NOT corrected. Three committed reference tables and half a smoke test were
computed from this file, so changing it changes what those tables mean. Pinned
in tests/test_structure_metrics.py so it cannot change quietly.

F-18  open    The NME cap of the MHC peptide is not a physical amide
---------------------------------------------------------------------
examples/mhc_peptide_ff14sb/tax_peptide_capped.pdb, residue NME 11. Planar and
with both bond lengths correct, but H and CH3 sit on the same side of the
nitrogen:

    C(VAL 10)-N-CH3   153.48 degrees      an sp2 amide nitrogen wants ~121
    C(VAL 10)-N-H     153.39 degrees      ~119
    H-N-CH3            53.12 degrees      ~119

NOT corrected. Rebuilding the cap means choosing coordinates that have not been
minimised against the force field. The fixture exists to exercise the staging
transform, which does not depend on the geometry being physical, and its README
says its energy is recorded rather than validated.

F-19  fixed   Forty arrays were allocated one way and grown another
--------------------------------------------------------------------
An array from new[] grown with realloc is undefined, and where it was also
released in a destructor it was three allocators for one block. NMEM_MIN is
100 and every shipped topology, structure and deck is longer, so the realloc
path ran on every job.

Ten files, not the five that were first counted: inter_setup.cpp alone held
more of it than all the readers together.

Invisible because alloc_dealloc_mismatch is off by default on macOS. With it
on, 14 of 14 committed inputs failed before the repair and 14 of 14 are clean
after. tests/sanitizer_panel.sh sets it explicitly and says why.

F-20  open    Two shipped decks cannot be regenerated from their sources
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
db_ol15_ol3 cannot be regenerated from the XML at all, and db_ol21_ol3's
tors_and_impr cannot either. db_ff14sb regenerates byte-identical. Established
by running the unmodified converter as a control, so it is not a consequence of
any change here.

A deck that cannot be regenerated cannot be checked against a newer release of
its source, and its provenance rests on a note rather than on a command.

F-21  fixed   The CMAP force was zero at a dihedral of exactly pi
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Found by sweeping the finite-difference step. Every energy term's residual
falls as the step squared, which is what a central difference must do when the
analytic force is the derivative. CMAP's did not move at all: 1.669519e-03
Ha/Bohr at a step of 1e-2 and 1.669858e-03 at 3e-7.

A residual that ignores the step is not discretisation error.

The force was pre = dVdphi/(sint*l123*l234), with sint clamped to 1e-8 when it
went below that. At phi = 0 or pi the two normals are parallel, so the
numerator vanishes as well and the true force is the finite limit of 0/0. The
clamp replaced the denominator and left the numerator, so the whole force came
out as exactly zero: 0 of 168 components on the ff19SB peptide.

A peptide built extended sits at phi = psi = pi on every residue. That is what
tleap writes, and it is what examples/peptide_ff19sb holds, so all four of its
CMAP terms sat on the singular point.

Proved by moving every atom by at most 0.01 Angstrom, which took the analytic
force from 0 non-zero components to 42 agreeing with the numerical one to
1.844e-10.

Replaced with the chain rule in the form that has no sin(phi) in it. Its only
singularity is three collinear atoms, which is real and already guarded. After
the repair the same structure gives 1.772e-08 at a step of 1e-05, and the sweep
converges at second order: fitted over the declared window of 1e-02 to 3e-04
Bohr, with no selection of rows, the exponent is 1.994.

The energy was never affected and still matches sander and OpenMM.

tests/cmap_force_driver.cpp already compared these forces to a finite
difference and passed: its geometry has no dihedral near 0 or pi. It now runs a
second geometry at exactly pi, which fails against the old code with 0.3998.

F-22  open    CMAP is checked on one system only
--------------------------------------------------
examples/peptide_ff19sb is the only committed input with a
\cmap_database_file, so it is the only structure the correction-map term is
measured on, at the energy level and at the force level. No staging input has
one at all, so the staging gradient of this term is not measured anywhere.

None of the six force fields validated here gives CMAP to a nucleic acid, so
this is a gap in coverage rather than a suspected defect. It is named because
a blank in coverage.txt must not read as a pass.

Closing it needs a second committed input carrying a correction map, and a
staging input carrying one.
