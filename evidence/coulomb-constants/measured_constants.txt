Precision
---------
sander prints EEL to four decimals, so its row above carries four
measured digits and two of formatting. The other three rows are
read from APIs and files that carry full double precision.

AMBER stores prmtop charges pre-multiplied by 18.2223, so sander's
constant is that number squared, exactly:
  18.2223^2 = 332.052217

That exact value is what results.txt corrects the sander column
with, not the four digits printed above. The two agree to the
precision sander reports: 332.0522 contains 332.052217.
