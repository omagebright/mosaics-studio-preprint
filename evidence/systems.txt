Systems
=======

Four structures are used. All are run in vacuum, dielectric 1, no cutoff,
no temperature, and no move.

  name        atoms  chains  contents                      coordinates
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1efs          818  2       13 DNA + 13 RNA, a duplex     examples/1efs_heteroduplex/1efs.pdb
                             with one strand of each
  peptide        56  1       ACE ALA GLY SER VAL NME       examples/peptide_ff14sb/
                                                           examples/peptide_ff19sb/
  dna_acgt      126  1       4 DNA, free 5' and 3' ends    examples/pure_terminal_controls/
  rna_acgu      127  1       4 RNA, free 5' and 3' ends    examples/pure_terminal_controls/

1efs is the only structure that mixes DNA and RNA. That is why it is the one
that exposed the torsion defect described in findings.txt: a parameter set
that is right for DNA and wrong for RNA shows up nowhere else.

The peptide has four residues between its two caps, of four different kinds.
That is deliberate. A peptide with one residue between the caps produces one
correction-map term, which resolves through a fallback and would pass whether
the map selection is right or wrong.

dna_acgt and rna_acgu are the pure terminal controls. They isolate the free
5' and 3' ends, which is where the residue templates of two force fields
differ. They carry no AMBER cross-engine energy comparison, which is what F-10
records; they are in the comparison in two other places. Both are checked at
the energy level against OpenMM under CHARMM36 nucleic, in
charmm36_dna_acgt.tsv and charmm36_rna_acgu.tsv, because a 5'-hydroxyl
terminus is the only one charmm36_2024.xml can both build and end a chain
with. Both are also swept at the force level under OL21/OL3, in forces/.
dna_acgt carries the geometric defect at DG 3 recorded as F-17.

The two structures that carry an AMBER reference are built by a committed
command, and the structure MOSAICS reads is the one the reference topology is
built from, never the other way round:

  1efs      tools/build_1efs_amber_reference.py --preset <force field>
  peptide   tools/build_protein_reference.py --sequence "ACE ALA GLY SER VAL NME"

dna_acgt and rna_acgu are committed as coordinates only, under
examples/pure_terminal_controls/. No command that generates them is recorded
here, so they are reproducible as files but not as a build.

The input given to the topology builder is committed next to each structure.
The builder's own screen output is not: it runs to several thousand lines of
which perhaps ten matter, and it is reproduced by rerunning the command.
