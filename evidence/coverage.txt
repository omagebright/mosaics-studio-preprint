Forces, analytic against a central difference of the same energy
----------------------------------------------------------------

The measurement is not the residual, it is the slope. A central difference is
wrong at order step squared, so a force that IS the derivative makes the
residual fall as the square as the step shrinks. A force wrong by a fixed
amount cannot: it stops falling at that amount. Tables in forces/.

slope is a least-squares fit of log(residual) on log(step) over a FIXED
window, declared before any curve was looked at: the four steps from 1e-02
down to 3e-04 Bohr, which every sweep spans, with no selection of rows. It is
not fitted on whichever rows happen to be falling. residual is the minimum of
the sweep, which is the quantity these tables report; it is neither the value
at the coarsest step nor the value at the finest.

Why the empty rows are empty
----------------------------

  F-07  OL24/OL3 needs three parameter files that its authors do not permit
        us to redistribute, so they are not in this repository and the row
        cannot be filled from a clone.

  F-08  CHARMM36 nucleic 5-prime-phosphate cannot be built from this XML.
        charmm36_2024.xml's 5PHO patch keeps the phosphate and cannot end a
        chain, so 1efs has no OpenMM reference. The 5-OH path (5TER/3TER) is
        measured: rna_acgu and dna_acgt vs OpenMM, leftover ~10^{-4} kcal/mol
        on electrostatics. Deck in params/db_charmm36_na. See charmm36.txt
        C-08, C-11.

  F-09  CHARMM36 for protein cannot be expressed at all in the current file
        format. Its correction maps are chosen partly by the residue that
        FOLLOWS the one being corrected. The format selects a map by the
        residue itself and has no way to say "unless the next one is
        proline". Fixing this changes the file format and the engine, so it
        is a decision, not a conversion.

  F-10  dna_acgt and rna_acgu were used only for the move-size check at the
        energy level. Both are measured at the force level above.

  F-21  The CMAP force was identically zero at a dihedral of exactly pi, and
        the ff19SB peptide is built extended so every one of its four CMAP
        terms sat there. Before the repair that row read 1.671e-03 with no
        slope at all. Fixed; see findings.txt.

  F-22  cmap is measured on one system only, because ff19SB is the only
        committed input with a \cmap_database_file, and no staging input has
        one at all. A nucleic force field with CMAP would need one; none of
        the six here has CMAP.

Force fields that pass here are correct on these structures at a single
geometry. That is not the same as correct.
