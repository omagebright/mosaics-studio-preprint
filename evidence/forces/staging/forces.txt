Analytic forces against a central difference of the energy
==========================================================

slope is d log(residual) / d log(step), a least-squares fit over
a FIXED window declared before any curve was looked at: the four
steps from 1e-02 down to 3e-04 Bohr, which every sweep spans,
with no selection of rows. A window chosen for already falling
as the square would test nothing.
A central difference is wrong at order step**2, so a correct
analytic force gives 2. A force that is wrong by a fixed
amount cannot: its residual stops falling at that amount.

residual is the largest |numerical - analytic| over every atom
and every component, in Hartree per Bohr.
